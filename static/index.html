Agreements and outcomes drift apart.

The promise, the delivery, and the amount someone asks to release stop matching. A shortfall can still be presented as the original figure. The people responsible are left reconstructing the story from inboxes and spreadsheets.

One record holds the accountability.

OUTCOME keeps what was agreed, the evidence of what happened, the exposure when they differ, and the decision that follows. A supplier order, a milestone, a repayment, a reimbursement, an expense, or an escrow-style release uses the same record. The 50-carton example shows the record. It is not the boundary of the product.

What is recorded, and what is not a payment.

This page is for people who want the public audit detail. It does not change a commitment, and connecting a wallet here does not authorise a payment.

Decisions and transfers

A recommendation is not an approval. An approval is recorded as APPROVED — NOT SUBMITTED unless a real transfer exists. Only a completed transfer may be called paid, and only with the network, the transaction hash, and an explorer link checked against the chain. This application has not recorded a completed transfer.

Signing in names a personal workspace. Signing an audit record writes a hash. Neither action submits a payment.

Explanations and extraction

An operational brief or an ordinary-language extraction is shown only after the cryptographic checks pass. HTTPS is not attestation. A failed check is not shown as verified. The deterministic assessment and a person’s approval remain authoritative. A brief never authorises a payment.

Before a brief is shown, OUTCOME preflights an UpToDate Intel TDX quote (debug bit clear, signing address and nonce bound, MRCONFIGID and RTMR3 checked) and then checks the EIP-191 ECDSA signature over the exact response bytes. A provider-TEE signature also requires an NVIDIA NRAS verdict of boolean true and a matching eat_nonce. A gateway signature is labelled separately and does not claim the model quote signed those bytes. Verification fails closed. The NRAS JWT signature, Sigstore provenance, and the live TLS peer certificate are not verified here.
